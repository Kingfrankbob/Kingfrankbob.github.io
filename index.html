  • A little about me

    Michael Cragun

    I'm a software developer who enjoys building games, tools, and experiments. Most of my work has focused in the area of Arduino and the tools that you can create with it including but not limited to ESP Wifi Modules, Data Loggers, MOSFETS and other control devices. I, along with a very close person to me have been able to make systems to alert when certain events happen, with websites that act as a front-end hosted by the arduino itself.
